TRACE-X runs a multi-hop BFS across the transaction graph, scores risk on 6 weighted factors, and attributes the final cash-out point to a named, regulated exchange with a confidence score — ready for a subpoena.

03 — KEY INNOVATIONS

What makes TRACE-X different

🎯 VASP Attribution

Not just "here's a graph" — TRACE-X names the exchange, the jurisdiction, and a confidence percentage, so officers know exactly where to send the freeze request.

🧠 AI Narrative

Every trace ends in a plain-English case summary, generated automatically — no more writing up the graph by hand for the FIR.

🛡 Stage-Proof by Design

A DEMO/LIVE adapter pattern means the platform never depends on a live RPC call to work — reliable in a courtroom or on a conference stage.
